About Era of Empires

Era of Empires is a strategy game management tool where you command nations, manage economies, wage wars, and forge alliances. Build your empire from nothing to global dominance.

Features include a battle calculator with tiered military units, a research progression system, territory capture and integration, war tracking with score events, and a full economic simulation.

This is version 1.0 of the game. Inspired by World Power, a more advanced strategy management tool.
